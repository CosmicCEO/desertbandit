DESERT
BANDIT

A six-reel slot-machine race across Anza-Borrego

What it is

Two to four players race their rigs along the old Desert Ranger board. Each turn you draw a goal card and roll six paper reels. Hold the reels that score, then roll the rest again for more, or bank what you have. Roll nothing that scores and the turn's stake is gone. The first bank to reach the victory total wins.

  • A lone bighorn sheep pays $100 and a lone Gambel's quail $50.
  • Three of a kind pays from $200 (sidewinders) to $1,000 (bighorn sheep); four, five and six of a kind pay two, three and four times that.
  • One of each creature on six reels is the Supersweep: $1,500.
  • Keep all six reels in one turn and you sweep: six fresh reels, the stake kept.

Any seat can be a computer player, so you can also play on your own.

Play money only

The dollars in this game are play money. Nothing is bought, sold or paid out, and there is no sign-up: a name and a room code are all it takes. The server rolls every reel, so a phone cannot change the result.

The creatures on the reels

Six real creatures of Anza-Borrego Desert State Park, drawn new for this game in the style of a field-guide plate.
